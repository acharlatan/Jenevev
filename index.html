Good to know

Little questions ♡

A few things to help you plan a cozy nail appointment.

Where do you travel?

Travel appointments depend on Jenevev’s availability and your location. Add your city and address to your plan so the trip can be discussed.

What should I expect for travel costs?

For a distance of 1–2 km, there is no distance charge; ₱80 covers the commute there and back. For over 2 km, the rate is ₱20 per km for travel there and back. Please confirm the final amount and how distance is measured with Jenevev before booking.

Can I bring a guest?

You can note whether you’d like to bring a guest in the planner. Please check with Jenevev before your appointment.

Can I share nail reference photos?

Yes! You can add up to three images to your plan. They stay on this device and are not sent from the website, so please share them with Jenevev when you contact her.

Can I request a custom design?

Absolutely. Choose the custom nail option, describe your idea, and add any inspiration pictures to your plan.

How much do the nail services cost?

Prices depend on the service and design. Jenevev can confirm the price, availability, and any travel fee with you directly.

Is my appointment confirmed when I save a plan?

No. Your plan stays on your device and is not sent. Your appointment is confirmed only after you and Jenevev agree on the details.
