# JENEVEV — brand and discovery foundation

## Project audit

**Known:** Jay (also known as Jenevev) has interests in painting, portraits, manga/anime, drawing and visual composition. The concept is based in the Philippines. The proposed weekly supply budget of ₱2,500–₱4,000 is exploratory, not committed. Geovanni Hernandez is supporting the brand, digital experience and marketing; he is not represented as owner of Jay's business.

**Unconfirmed:** Jay's nail training, qualifications, service capabilities, commercial readiness, target city, premises, service area, prices, contacts, social handles, audience demand and dates. This is a creative concept, not an operating studio. Do not publish claims or accept clients until verified.

**Immediate priorities:** identify safe training and practice requirements; talk with potential customers; build an authentic body of practice work with consent; validate operating costs and local requirements; establish a genuine contact channel before opening inquiries. The site is a concept-stage creative experience, not a booking system.

## Positioning

For people who treat beauty as a personal creative detail, JENEVEV is an emerging artist-led nail-art concept exploring illustration, color and expressive compositions on a miniature canvas. Its difference is a sketchbook-first point of view shaped by Jay's visual-art interests. The immediate offer is a creative journey and inspiration; services are conditional on training, safety and founder confirmation.

**Mission:** Make room for personal expression in the smallest details.  
**Vision:** Build a thoughtful creative practice where original ideas can become wearable art.  
**Values:** curiosity, considered craft, personal expression, honesty about the process, and care for people.

**Voice:** observant, warm, concise, slightly playful. Write like a real person sharing an idea. Use specific images and plain language. Avoid inflated claims, invented intimacy, excessive punctuation, “girlboss” language and generic luxury clichés. Filipino-English variants should be tested with actual audiences, not performed as stereotypes.

## Identity system

- **Wordmark:** JENEVEV in a tightly spaced high-contrast serif; the website uses a typographic treatment, not a registered logo. Do not use a registration symbol without verification.
- **Secondary mark:** JENEVEV / an independent creative practice, set in compact uppercase sans.
- **Compact mark:** a simple asterisk, used as punctuation, never as a claim of a unique trademark. A custom J monogram may be developed later.
- **Palette:** Paper `#F3F0E8`, Ink `#25251F`, Oxblood `#8C282F`, Stone `#DED9CE`, dusty rose `#C9B6AA`. Ink on paper is the default for readable text; white on oxblood or ink for strong contrast. Verify contrast when combinations or type sizes change.
- **Typography:** Cormorant Garamond for display and italic accents; DM Sans for functional copy. Both are distributed under the SIL Open Font License; files are locally bundled. Georgia and system sans are fallbacks.
- **Motifs:** stars, fine rules, numbered captions, loose annotations and paper edges. Use sparingly; the artwork remains the focus.
- **Illustration:** original marks and character concepts; avoid copying protected characters or branded artwork. Personal fandom inspiration does not automatically confer reproduction rights.
- **Photography:** directional daylight or direct flash, close crops, real texture and visible human detail. Show full design and a scale reference as well as editorial crops. Label concepts as concepts.
- **Social layouts:** one clear focal point, generous margins, small recurring identifier and a readable caption. Alternate close-up, sketchbook, founder and process formats. Do not make every post a template.
- **Packaging concept:** uncoated cream backing card, one-color ink wordmark, small oxblood stamp, and a handwritten care note when real products and care instructions exist. Test costs and product safety before ordering.
- **Menu concept:** a single-page sheet with confirmed service name, scope, time, price, additions, preparation and conditions. Mark unapproved entries as internal drafts; none are public now.
- **Do:** use authentic process imagery, clear status labels, permissioned work and accessible contrast. **Don't:** simulate client testimonials, imply credentials, use stock images as Jay's portfolio, or let decoration obscure the action.

## Discovery plan

No current competitor or consumer research was verified for this project; there is no confirmed target city and no browser/search source was available in this session. Do not treat these hypotheses or proposed prices as Philippine market facts. Once a service area is confirmed, document 5–8 relevant artists/studios using public profiles: date observed, URL, location, specialty, published price and scope, booking steps, evidence of hygiene, image treatment, and what they do well. Do not infer sales from likes or invent a price where none is published. Compare direct competitors and adjacent independent illustrators, then revisit positioning.

Interview 8–12 potential customers across the above segments before deciding on a service mix. Recruit voluntarily, disclose the early-stage concept, avoid leading questions, and record only consented notes. Ask: (1) Tell me about the last time you chose nail art or decided not to. (2) Where did you discover the artist? (3) What made you trust them? (4) What would make a design feel worth its price? (5) What information would you need before making a request? (6) Which of these original sketches feels most like you, and why? (7) How would you feel about a press-on versus an appointment? (8) What would make you hesitate? (9) How do you prefer to contact a maker? (10) What would you expect for timing and aftercare? Record contradictions and exact anonymous language; don't infer demand from compliments.

**Testable opportunities:** people may save a sketch-to-concept story more readily than a finished image; clear concept labels may increase trust; some customers may prefer a small number of distinctive, attainable motifs over unlimited customization. None has been validated. Enduring needs to investigate include safety, trust, value, self-expression and reliable communication. Chrome, aura, sculptural effects and character-inspired ideas are optional aesthetics, not mandatory capabilities; adopt only after practical training and demand evidence.
