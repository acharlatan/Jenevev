# JENEVEV — Building an Art-Driven Beauty Brand From Zero

## Overview

JENEVEV is an early-stage Philippine nail-art concept associated with Jay, whose interests include painting, portrait illustration, manga and drawing. The opportunity is to build a recognizable independent creative practice around expressive miniature art while being honest that the commercial studio, credentials, location and service menu are not yet confirmed.

**Roles:** Jay is the founder and source of the artistic interests that inform the concept. Geovanni Hernandez is supporting brand strategy, marketing, digital experience and potential launch. The current project creates a concept and digital prototype; it does not claim that either person has delivered a commercial service or achieved market results.

## Opportunity and research

The strategic opportunity is to make a sketchbook-led point of view recognizable across an eventual customer journey. Three audience segments, competitor comparison criteria and interview questions are hypotheses in `01-brand-and-research.md`. No local competitor study, customer survey or current Philippine pricing research was verified here. Those are important next steps once a market is confirmed.

## Strategy and creative direction

Three directions were considered: Art School After Hours, Digital Dollhouse and Miniature Masterpieces. Art School After Hours was selected for its fit with Jay's drawing interests and accessible production methods, softened by gallery-like restraint. Ink, paper and oxblood; expressive serif typography; numbered captions; and a small star motif form a flexible system. The positioning: an emerging artist-led practice exploring illustration and individuality on a tiny canvas.

## Digital experience

The working static site offers a mobile-first home, a labeled concept gallery with filters and details, artist introduction, pre-launch service status, journal, contact draft, moodboard with local saving and download, and a transparent category-based design discovery tool. The user journey moves from discovery to exploration to a saved inspiration summary. The site does not send inquiries, take bookings, collect signups or run analytics. AI-generated conceptual imagery is explicitly labeled and must not be described as Jay's completed work.

## Marketing and execution

The launch playbook includes five content pillars, a 30-day calendar, short-form and community concepts, a customer journey, metrics, and an experiment backlog. Financial scenarios are explicitly illustrative and denominated in Philippine pesos. The phased operating checklist prioritizes confirmed skills, safety, local requirements and truthful communication.
